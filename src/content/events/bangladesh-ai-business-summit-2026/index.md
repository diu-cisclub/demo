Two projects built by club members reached the national Top 20 at the Bangladesh AI Business Summit 2026.

## EON
An offline, AI-powered business assistant. It works without internet, external APIs or cloud AI: it connects to existing business systems, analyses live data, finds hidden errors and explains them in Bangla, English or Banglish, while business data stays on local servers.

Team: Md. Imran Hossain, Nafisa Tabassum Nusrat, Shumaiya Jaman Shimu.

## Kupi (কুপি)
An AI learning platform from Team ImpactX Lab that makes study more personal with AI teachers, interactive classes and visual learning. Mentor: Abdullah Bin Kasem Bhuiyan.

Team: Md. Tanvir Hannan Anik, Jobaied Chowdhury Akash, MD. Mahid Alam Marin, Nusrat Jahan Jitu, Talbiyah Ahmad Ramal.
