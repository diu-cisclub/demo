Competing in the Programmable Line Robot Competition (Senior category), four club members won the **silver medal**, finishing second in the national qualifier organised by Tech Autocrats.

Team: Fayek Ahanaf, Nafisa Tabassum Nusrat, Shakila Akter, Md. Tawfiqur Rahman Srijon.

The result earned them a place at the World Robot Games 2026 International Round at Chiba Port Arena, Japan, in November 2026, themed “RoboBuild: Engineering Sustainable Cities”.
