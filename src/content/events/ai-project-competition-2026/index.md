Organised by the Department of CIS with DIU CIS Club, the AI Project Competition invited students to turn AI ideas into real solutions, for a grand prize of **৳5 lakh**. Proposals closed on 20 April 2026.

## What the judges looked for
- Smart student support and academic assistance systems
- AI in healthcare, education or daily life
- Intelligent automation for institutional services
- Data-driven decision-making tools
- Any creative AI solution to a real problem

The final was attended by the Honourable Adviser to the Ministry of Posts, Telecommunications and Information Technology.
