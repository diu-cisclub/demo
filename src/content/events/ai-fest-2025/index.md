AI Fest 2025, organised by the Department of CIS with the club’s members and executives running it, was the department’s AI and programming festival.

## Tracks
- **Programming Contest**: for first-semester students
- **PromptX**: a prompt-engineering contest for seniors
- **Project Showcase**: seniors present what they built

Participants received an event T-shirt, food, a certificate and prize money.
