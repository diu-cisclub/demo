The club partnered in organising the 4th DIU Data Science Summit, themed “AI in Education”.

The programme included a Data Hackathon, a hands-on workshop, idea poster presentations, a project competition and the **PromptCraft Challenge**. It was organised by DIU’s Software Engineering Department, the Data Science Lab and partner clubs.
