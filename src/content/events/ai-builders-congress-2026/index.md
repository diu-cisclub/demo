The club invites members to the International AI Builders Congress 2026, a programme about turning AI ideas into products people can use. Challenges span 11 domains, from healthcare and education to agriculture, climate and finance.

## Two ways to take part
- **Idea to Unicorn**: form a team of 3–5 and take a solution from idea to prototype to product.
- **Zero to Launch**: an individual, one-hour AI application-building challenge on Congress day.

Registration is free. Team preliminary registration closed on 31 October 2026.
