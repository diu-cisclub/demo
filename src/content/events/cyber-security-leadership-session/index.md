Dr. Monjur Ahmed, a visiting researcher of the Department of CIS, spoke about cyber security and the reality of security leadership.

He is Dean at the Australasian Academy of Higher Education, Australia, and was previously Head of Cyber Security at Hamilton City Council, New Zealand. He holds a PhD in cyber security from Auckland University of Technology.
